## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

Our proposed solution is FarmBridge AI, a smart digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers and bulk buyers.

Farmers can register on the platform and upload their available crops, quantities and prices. Consumers and retailers can directly purchase products from farmers without depending on multiple middlemen.

The platform also provides AI-based demand forecasting to help farmers understand which crops are in demand. A smart logistics system helps identify suitable delivery routes and reduce transportation costs.

Unique Features:

Direct farmer-to-consumer connection.

Transparent product prices.

AI-based demand prediction.

Smart delivery and route optimization.

Online ordering and payment facilities.

Live order and delivery tracking.

## Technical Approach

Technologies to be Used:

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Backend: Python with Django.

Database: MySQL.

AI/ML: Python, Pandas and Scikit-learn.

Maps and Routes: Google Maps API.

Payment: Secure digital payment integration.

Implementation Methodology:

Farmers register and upload their agricultural products.

Consumers and bulk buyers browse available products.

Buyers compare prices and place orders.

AI predicts future demand using previous sales data.

The system connects orders with suitable delivery services.

Route optimization helps reduce delivery time and transportation costs.

Farmers receive payments and buyers receive their products.

Working Flow:



Farmer Registration → Product Upload → Digital Marketplace → Buyer Orders → AI Demand Prediction → Logistics and Route Optimization → Product Delivery.

## Feasibility and Viability

The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses commonly available technologies and smartphones.

Potential Challenges:

Farmers may have limited digital knowledge.

Transportation costs may be high.

Product availability may change frequently.

Internet connectivity may be limited in rural areas.

Solutions to Overcome Challenges:

Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

Support regional languages.

Allow FPOs to manage registrations and orders.

Use AI to predict demand and reduce wastage.

Connect nearby farmers and buyers to reduce transportation costs.

Provide SMS notifications for important updates.

The platform can initially be implemented in one village or district and gradually expanded to other regions.
